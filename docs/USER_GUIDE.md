# Hive Salon — Zero-Training User Guide & Front-Desk Staff Manual

## 1. Quick Start for New Front-Desk Receptionists

Welcome to Hive Salon! You can run the entire front-desk operation using this 9-step standard operating procedure. No technical training is required.

---

## 2. The 9-Step Daily Client Visit Lifecycle

```
[1. Find Customer] ➔ [2. Create Appointment] ➔ [3. Check In] ➔ [4. Assign Stylist]
         │
         ▼
[5. Complete Service] ➔ [6. POS Invoice] ➔ [7. Accept Payment] ➔ [8. Send Receipt] ➔ [9. Rebook]
```

### Step 1: Find Customer (`Ctrl + K`)
- Press `Ctrl + K` anywhere on your screen to open the Search Palette.
- Type the customer's phone number or name to open their **Customer 360 Profile**.
- *If new customer*: Click **+ New Customer** (`Alt + C`) to register them in under 15 seconds.

### Step 2: Create Appointment (`/appointments`)
- Navigate to the **Appointment Diary**.
- Click any open time slot under the requested stylist or chair.
- Select the service (e.g. *Artisan Balayage* or *Hydra-Facial*) and click **Confirm Booking**.
- The client receives an automated SMS and WhatsApp confirmation.

### Step 3: Check In Customer Upon Arrival
- When the guest enters the salon, locate their appointment and click **Check In**.
- This updates the live dashboard chair occupancy and notifies the stylist.

### Step 4: Assign Stylist & Chair
- Verify that the primary stylist and any secondary assistants are correctly credited on the booking.

### Step 5: Complete Service
- Once the treatment is finished, click **Mark Completed**.

### Step 6: Create POS Invoice (`/pos`)
- Click **Checkout** on the appointment.
- All service lines, prices, 18% GST, and stylist commissions are loaded automatically.
- Any active membership discounts, prepaid wallet balances, or loyalty points are applied automatically.

### Step 7: Accept Payment & Split Tenders
- If the customer wishes to split payment (e.g. ₹2,000 via UPI + ₹2,500 via Card), enter each tender amount and click **Record Payment**.

### Step 8: Send Digital Receipt
- Click **Send WhatsApp Invoice** or click **Print Slip** for a 3-inch luxury thermal receipt.

### Step 9: Rebook Next Visit
- Schedule the client's next 45-day color refresh or trim maintenance before they leave.
